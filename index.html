Straight answers about working with BlueKite

What is BlueKite?

BlueKite is an AI automation agency in Vancouver, BC. It takes repetitive work off marketing agencies and IT consultants in Canada and the US, like answering new enquiries, following up and writing proposals, while a person on your team approves anything important.

What can BlueKite automate for my business?

Most of the repetitive work around winning and serving clients: answering new enquiries, booking calls and reminders, following up, moving details between your apps, building contact lists, writing proposals and pulling reports together. If something isn't worth automating yet, you'll hear that on the free call.

How long does it take to set up?

About 14 days from the day you approve your plan. It starts with a free 30-minute call, your written plan follows within a few days, and everything is built inside the tools you already use. If it isn't live in 14 days, you get every dollar back.

Will my clients know it's AI?

Only if you want them to. Replies are written in your team's voice from how you already talk to clients, and anything sensitive waits for a person to approve before it goes out. Most people just notice that you got back to them quickly.

Is our data safe?

Everything runs inside the accounts you already own. The AI only sees what a task needs, and your information is never used to train public models. Everything follows Canada's privacy law (PIPEDA) and anti-spam law (CASL). When data is especially sensitive, it can stay on a private AI that runs on your own systems.

Do we have to change how we work?

No. It works inside Outlook, Teams, Gmail, Slack and the CRM you already use. There's no new app to learn, and your team gets a short walkthrough before anything goes live.

How much does it cost?

Each project is a fixed price, agreed in writing before any work starts, plus optional monthly care to keep it running and improving. Your free automation plan shows the exact price for your business, and if we miss the result, you get your money back.

What happens if it doesn't deliver?

If your automation isn't live within 14 days of approving your plan, you get a full refund. Just ask, and the money is back with you.

What if something breaks?

Every step is logged, so problems are easy to spot and fix. If an AI service ever goes down, the work goes to a person on your team, and they're told straight away.

Is cold email allowed?

Yes, when it's done properly. We only contact businesses at addresses they publish themselves, keep every message relevant to the person's role, and include who's sending it and an easy way to opt out.
