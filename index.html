Questions people usually ask

How is BlueKite different from other AI agencies?

We start by looking at how work moves through your team. Then we automate only the steps worth automating and leave the rest alone. You see working demos before you spend anything.

We tried AI tools before and they didn't stick. Why would this be different?

Usually the tool was one more app someone had to remember to open. We build into the inbox, CRM and ticketing system your team already uses, and check every action ourselves for the first few weeks.

How long does it take?

About 14 days from when you approve your plan. It starts with a free 30-minute call, and your written plan arrives a few days later.

How much does it cost?

Each project has a fixed price, agreed in writing before we start, with optional monthly care after. If it isn't live within 14 days of approving your plan, you get a full refund.

Is our data safe?

Yes. Your data stays in the systems you already pay for, and none of it trains public models. We follow Canada's privacy law (PIPEDA) and anti-spam law (CASL).

Is cold email allowed?

Yes, done right. We only email addresses businesses publish themselves, keep every message relevant to the person's role, and always include who's sending it and how to opt out.
