    Questions, answered

    Everything you need to know before your first fix goes live.

    We spend 30 minutes on how work moves through your firm and where it stalls. Afterwards you get a written plan with three fixes, each with a fixed price. No prep needed and no obligation.

    Every firm’s tools and volume are different, so each fix is priced for you. The price is fixed and agreed in writing before any work starts. It does not grow by the hour.

    The ones you already use: Outlook, Gmail and Microsoft 365, Clio, QuickBooks Online, Xero, TaxDome, Karbon, DocuSign, ConnectWise, HaloPSA, Autotask and more. If we can’t work inside a tool, we’ll say so on the call.

    Within 14 days of you approving the written plan. If it isn’t live by then, the fee is refunded in full.

    Nothing breaks. Your firm owns every account and everything we built, and it keeps running. Monthly care is optional.
