Account health radar

Every client checked for the quiet signs before a cancellation: ticket spikes, falling satisfaction, a main contact who left, skipped reviews, unopened reports and late invoices, weighed against the renewal date. You get the few to call this week, the growth openings, and the email and review agenda for each call.

Clients rarely say they are unhappy. They just stop renewing.

The signs are scattered across the ticketing system, survey scores, the inbox and the books, and nobody joins them up until the cancellation email arrives.

Clear rules find the risk; you get the call plan.

Each account gets points for each warning sign, with the reason shown, so you can see exactly why it is flagged. Then it suggests the next move and drafts the check-in email and a review agenda that opens with your wins.
